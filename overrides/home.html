Built for Production, Not Toy Demos

RingBufferBook

Flat tick-indexed ring with 16-byte POD slots, occupancy bitmap + __builtin_ctzll depth walks, lazy OS page commitment, and runtime window sizing (19-bit CEX / 10-bit Polymarket).

Streaming State Machines + ONNX

7 bundled C++ state machines (including six_venue_order and global_bba) shared across historical_feature_gen, backtest_runner, and live_infra with in-process ONNX Runtime inference.

Realistic PaperExchange

Evaluates orders as taker at ack + latency, fills resting remainders as maker against quotes and subsequent prints, and stops fills immediately on cancel. Polymarket backtests also model queue notional.

Crash-Safe QuoteHolds

Quote pulls are durable state—not transient commands—so a restarted engine comes back with resting entries still pulled, backed by a quote_guard staleness heartbeat and graceful SIGTERM sweeps.

Zero-Fork Plugin Registry

Keep proprietary alpha in your own repo. Register strategies with KERONIS_REGISTER_STRATEGY and state machines with KERONIS_REGISTER_STATE_MACHINE via -DKERONIS_STRATEGY_DIRS.

MPFR Precision & Native Venues

Clean-room Apache-2.0 mpreal over shared-linked MPFR/GMP, strict -fno-fast-math, native clients for Hyperliquid (Rust signer), Aster, and Polymarket, plus Lighter L2 books.
